USP vs. ABB / Siemens Field Services
Nordic execution specialists, not global product vendors
Global players sell systems and send in service teams. Enersense delivers — with deep local presence, field expertise, and accountability for the full project, from design to commissioning.
Field execution Local accountability vs. product + service
USP vs. Eltel / Quanta Services
Energy transition + connectivity under one roof
Pure network builders focus on one vertical. Enersense spans power infrastructure, energy transition, and telecom connectivity — giving clients a single partner as their asset needs evolve.
Cross-sector scope Energy + connectivity vs. single vertical
USP vs. NCC / YIT Industrial
Critical infrastructure specialist, not general construction
General contractors build anything. Enersense builds what matters most — high-voltage lines, substations, wind farms, hydrogen facilities — with the technical depth that critical assets demand.
Technical specialisation Critical assets vs. general build
USP vs. AFRY / Rejlers / Pöyry
Advisory that builds — not just plans
Engineering consultants produce plans and recommendations. Enersense produces completed infrastructure. The combination of expert advisory and hands-on delivery closes the gap from strategy to live asset.
Deliver, not just advise Lifecycle ownership vs. consultancy-only
